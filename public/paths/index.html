Path

Two objects, and what connects them

Resemblances follows a chain: each step is among the nearest look-alikes of the one before, and the path takes as many small steps as it needs rather than one large leap. The space between two objects grows as they look less alike.

Straight line draws the direct route between the two in the model's space and asks, at evenly spaced points along it, which object sits nearest. Objects hang below the line by how far they sit from that point — the collection's way of filling a gap it may not really have.

What it cannot know. The model compares pictures. It will connect objects through a shared backdrop or a similar crop as readily as through form or material. It is a measurement of photographs, not a judgement about design history, and it is worth disagreeing with.
